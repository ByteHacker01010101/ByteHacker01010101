## Brand icons

**AtCoder:** no AtCoder mark exists in Simple Icons, and the official logo could not be downloaded in the build environment. The AtCoder tile is a plain "AC" text placeholder, not the official logo. Replace it with the official mark from https://atcoder.jp if you want one.
